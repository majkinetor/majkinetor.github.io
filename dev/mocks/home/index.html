  3. 03Numbers firstThree figures read live from projects.json, links underneath.
  4. 04Timeline sparklineA strip chart of projects running at once, 2000 to now, linking to the notebook.
  5. 05Man pageFormatted as MILIC(1): NAME, SYNOPSIS, DESCRIPTION, FILES, SEE ALSO.
  6. 06Notebook cellOne query cell, milic.whoami, in the same style as the projects notebook.
  7. 07Now splitName and links on the left, a short "Now" list on the right.
  8. 08Directory treetree ~ output, every branch a link.
  9. 09Big typeA huge name, one sentence, three large links.
  10. 10ChangelogName and tagline, then a dated list of recent updates.
